# Antidugg — hyllekort A5: prompt do Gemini (2026-11-18)

Hotový návrh na tlač je `02_PRACA/grafika/antidugg_hylle_a5.pdf` (vektor, ostrý).
Prompt nižšie je **alternatíva**, ak chce Luk fotorealistický vzhľad. Pozor: AI generátory
často pokazia nórske texty (å, ø) — texty po vygenerovaní skontrolovať písmeno po písmene,
alebo nechať Gemini vygenerovať len obrázok BEZ textu (prompt B) a texty doplní agent.

## Prompt A — celý plagát s textom
Create a clean, modern A5 portrait retail shelf card (148 × 210 mm, print quality, 300 dpi)
for an eco-friendly anti-fog spray called "Isokor ANTIDUGG". Style: Scandinavian, minimal,
white background, accent colour deep burgundy (#8A0B3F), light sage-green hexagon shapes
as subtle decoration. Layout from top to bottom:
1. Top: wide burgundy band with big white bold condensed text "ANTIDUGG".
   Under it in burgundy: "Slutt på dugg – klar sikt!"
2. Hero image: a pair of glasses (or a car windscreen) split in the middle – left half
   completely fogged up with condensation droplets, labelled "UTEN"; right half crystal
   clear showing a sunny Norwegian landscape, labelled "MED ANTIDUGG".
3. Heading "Brukes på" with 6 simple burgundy line icons in sage circles:
   "Bilruter", "Briller", "Speil", "Visir og masker", "Vinduer", "Plast og pleksiglass".
4. Heading "Slik gjør du" with 4 numbered steps in burgundy circles:
   1 "Rengjør og avfett", 2 "Spray og fordel tynt", 3 "Vent 3 min – poler",
   4 "Klar sikt i 2–6 uker".
5. Bottom strip: "Test det: pust på glasset!"
Plenty of white space, large readable text, no clutter, no extra words, flat vector look.

## Prompt B — len obrázok (bez textu), texty doplní agent
Photorealistic close-up of a car windscreen from inside on a cold Norwegian winter morning,
split exactly in the middle: the left half completely fogged with fine condensation and
water droplets, the right half perfectly clear showing a snowy road and pine trees in soft
morning sun. Clean, bright, high detail, natural colours, no text, no logos, no people,
landscape format 3:2.
